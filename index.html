The AI part

Three opinions on every plant — not one black box

Most plant apps hand you a name and hope you don't ask follow-ups. Specia runs every find through a trust ladder, and shows you each rung.

Opinion 1 · Vision engine

Science-grade plant vision

The open Pl@ntNet computer-vision engine — used by researchers and citizen-science projects — scores your photos (tag leaves, flowers or bark for extra accuracy) against tens of thousands of species. Actual confidence score, not a vibe.

Opinion 2 · Second AI

A second AI that can overrule the first

When confidence is shaky, a second AI looks at your actual photo and issues its own verdict — agree, or disagree and nominate the right species. You see both opinions and can switch with one tap.

Beyond the name · Insights

AI Insights, written per find

Native range, care notes, toxicity warnings and one genuinely interesting fact — generated fresh for the species you actually photographed, cached on the observation so it's yours forever.

  • Rescue path: if both engines strike out, a vision-model fallback still attempts to name the plant — your first-ever photo is never a dead end.
  • Bring your own model: power users can plug their own OpenAI-compatible key; the key never leaves your device.
  • And the third opinion is a human: when even the second AI isn't confident enough, the situation below takes over.
